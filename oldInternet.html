Book Design — Web History in Print

web archeology: a physical archive

A printed book that excavates the visual and cultural remnants of the early internet — preserving the aesthetics, vernacular, and feeling of a web that no longer exists.

Year 2025
Role Book Design, Editorial Design, Research
Tools InDesign, Illustrator, Wayback Machine
Type Book Design — Print

The early internet had a texture to it — animated GIFs, tiled backgrounds, blinking marquee text, neon on black. It was built by amateurs with something to say, and you could feel that in every pixel. web archeology is an attempt to hold onto that before it disappears entirely.

The book works like a dig site. Each spread is organized around an artifact — a geocities page, a forum thread, an AIM away message — laid out with the visual language of the era intact: low-res screenshots, original color palettes, typefaces that were cutting-edge in 1998. Surrounding each artifact is context: what platform it came from, what moment it lived in, what it tells us about who people were becoming online.

"The early web was a place people built by hand. This book is a way of saying: someone was here, and it mattered."

Design Approach

The editorial design walks a deliberate line — the book's layout is refined and contemporary, while the artifacts inside are presented raw, unpolished, and faithful to the original. The contrast between the two creates a kind of museum effect: the chaos is preserved, but framed with intention.

Typography alternates between a clean serif for body text and period-accurate monospace for captions and artifact labels, reinforcing the dialogue between then and now. Color is drawn directly from the source material — every chapter takes its palette from the dominant hues of the era it documents.

Research

Sourcing material meant hours in the Wayback Machine, tracing dead links back to cached versions of pages that haven't loaded in twenty years. The research process became its own kind of archaeology — and that sense of excavation, of careful recovery, is baked into how the book is structured and sequenced.
